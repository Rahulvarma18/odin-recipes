Lasanga

Home

Description

Lasanga is a hearty, indulgent Italian dish that's beloved around the world. This baked casserole consists of layers of wide, flat pasta sheets, savory meat sauce, creamy ricotta or béchamel, and plenty of gooey melted cheese. The meat sauce is typically made with ground beef or sausage, simmered in rich tomato sauce with onions, garlic, and Italian herbs.

Ingredients

  • 9 lasagna noodles
  • 3 500g ground beef or Italian sausage
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 700ml tomato sauce or marinara
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 2 cups ricotta or béchamel sauce

Steps

  1. Cook lasagna noodles, drain and set aside.
  2. In a pan, cook onion and garlic, add meat and cook until browned.
  3. Add tomato sauce, seasoning, salt, and pepper. Simmer for 20 minutes.
  4. In a baking dish, spread a layer of meat sauce, then noodles, then ricotta/béchamel. Repeat layers.
