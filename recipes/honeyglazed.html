Honey Glazed Chicken

Home

Description

Honey Glazed Chicken is a delightful fusion of sweet and savory flavors. This dish features succulent chicken pieces pan-seared to perfection and then coated in a rich, glossy glaze made from honey, soy sauce, garlic, and a touch of ginger. The honey caramelizes slightly in the pan, creating a sticky coating that clings to every piece of chicken, enhancing both the taste and texture.

Ingredients

  • 500g chicken thighs or breasts (boneless)
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 3 cloves garlic (minced)
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Spring onions (for garnish)

Steps

  1. Season the chicken with salt and pepper.
  2. In a pan, heat olive oil and cook chicken until golden brown and fully cooked.
  3. In a bowl, mix honey, soy sauce, garlic, and ginger.
  4. Pour the mixture into the pan and simmer for 5-7 minutes until it thickens. Use cornstarch mixed with a little water if needed.
